Paper claims describe their original problems; applicability to this project's 58-row tabular data is our inference. No paper below establishes a bonded-PDMS law for this dataset.

## Physical background and broader framing

[Karniadakis et al. (2021), Physics-informed machine learning, Nature Reviews Physics 3, 422-440](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-021-00314-5) provides a taxonomy for data/physics integration. Use it to distinguish constrained empirical prediction from governing-equation inference. This is a review, not primary evidence for a particular adsorption mechanism.

[Quere (1999), Fluid coating on a fiber, Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics 31, 347-384](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.fluid.31.1.347) reviews coating regimes and geometry effects. Fiber results require care when applied to plates. [Surfactant effects in the Landau-Levich problem (2006), Journal of Fluid Mechanics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/abs/surfactant-effects-in-the-landaulevich-problem/BA347E2E8AD91B764223DF7EE79E013C) is primary evidence that interfacial physics can modify classical coating behavior. These sources motivate regime checks; they do not justify an LLD residual on dry bonded thickness.

## Review synthesis

Prioritize the compact tanh network with a transparent bound, then isolate smoothness and any evidence-based monotonicity using ablations. Next compare a gated/modified MLP and loss balancing if optimization diagnostics justify them. Bayesian inference is a useful extension once measurement noise is known. Fourier features require evidence of multiscale response. DeepONet and FNO require a materially different data representation.

Evaluate candidate architectures on the same grouped and extrapolation splits, repeated seeds, violation metrics, and training-only data-efficiency subsets. Choose models using validation, then freeze the choice before an independent experimental test. Physics constraints can improve plausibility without improving predictive error; report both outcomes.
